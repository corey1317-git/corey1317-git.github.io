Put Home Vault on your phone

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open thorpetech.org/home_vault.html in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open the page in Chrome
  2. Tap Install when prompted, or the ⋮ menu → Install app

It opens full-screen with its own icon and works offline. Turn on Settings → Sync between phones so you both see the same plan.
